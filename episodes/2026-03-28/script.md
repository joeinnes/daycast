source: BBC News
previously_covered: true
update_note: Jury delivers verdict with six million dollars in damages; previous coverage reported only on the UK's planned social media bans and digital curfews for teenagers

A California jury has found Meta and YouTube liable on all counts in what is being called a landmark case on social media addiction. The plaintiff, a twenty-year-old woman who began using YouTube at six and Instagram at nine, was awarded six million dollars in combined compensatory and punitive damages. Hundreds of similar cases are now in the pipeline, and the ruling could force significant changes to how these platforms handle younger users.
